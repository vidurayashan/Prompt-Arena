Activity 2 Chain-of-thought prompting
+15 pts
Direct vs step-by-step prompts — which do you trust more?
Scenario: A subscription costs $30 per month. For months 1 to 3, there is a 15% discount. There is also a one-time $10 setup fee. After discounts and fees, apply 10% GST. What is the total cost for the first 3 months?
1Explore direct prompt: Prompt the AI to give only the final total in dollars. Do not ask for any steps.
2Explore step-by-step prompt: Prompt the AI to think step by step, show each calculation, then give the final total in dollars on the last line.
3Reflect: Which output did you trust more and why? When might you prefer the other method?

Activity 7 Tree-of-thought prompting
+15 pts
Strategic decision using tree-of-thought (ToT)
How ToT works: Think of a leadership team deciding on a new office location — first they explore possible regions, then narrow down by considering rent, access and team preferences, then select the most suitable site. ToT mirrors this: it opens options, analyses them and selects the best fit.
1Choose a business scenario that requires comparing multiple options.
2Prompt the AI to explore each option as a separate branch, evaluate them, then recommend the best.
3Reflect: How did ToT produce a different result from a simple direct prompt?
